## Section 4: Prompt Libraries & Templates

### PromptBase
- **Description:** The leading marketplace for buying and selling AI prompts
- **What's in it:** 100,000+ prompts for ChatGPT, Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, and more
- **Link:** promptbase.com
- **Best for:** Finding niche-specific prompts, selling your own prompts, understanding what people pay for

### Awesome ChatGPT Prompts (GitHub)
- **Description:** Community-curated collection of role-based ChatGPT prompts
- **What's in it:** 200+ persona-based prompts (Act as a Linux terminal, Act as a financial advisor, etc.)
- **Link:** github.com/f/awesome-chatgpt-prompts
- **Best for:** Quick role prompts, inspiration, understanding the "Act as" technique

### ShareGPT
- **Description:** Platform for sharing and discovering full ChatGPT conversations
- **What's in it:** Real conversations showing effective prompting in action
- **Link:** sharegpt.com
- **Best for:** Seeing multi-turn prompting, learning from real examples

### OpenAI Prompt Engineering Guide
- **Description:** Official guide from OpenAI on effective prompting techniques
- **What's in it:** Six core strategies with examples, including CoT, few-shot, and system prompts
- **Link:** platform.openai.com/docs/guides/prompt-engineering
- **Best for:** Authoritative reference, understanding official best practices

### Anthropic Prompt Library
- **Description:** Claude's official collection of example prompts across categories
- **What's in it:** 50+ ready-to-use prompts for coding, writing, analysis, and more
- **Link:** docs.anthropic.com/claude/prompt-library
- **Best for:** Claude-optimized prompting, quality reference examples

### Learn Prompting (learnprompting.org)
- **Description:** Free, open-source prompt engineering course and reference
- **What's in it:** Comprehensive guide from basics to advanced techniques with examples
- **Link:** learnprompting.org
- **Best for:** Structured learning, intermediate to advanced techniques

### AIPRM for ChatGPT
- **Description:** Chrome extension adding thousands of community prompts directly in ChatGPT

## Section 6: Books Worth Reading

1. **"The Coming Wave" — Mustafa Suleyman (2023)**
   - *Why Read:* Co-founder of DeepMind explains AI's trajectory and risks. Essential perspective on where AI is heading and what it means for society and business.

2. **"Prompt Engineering for Generative AI" — James Phoenix & Mike Taylor (2024)**
   - *Why Read:* The first dedicated textbook on prompt engineering. Covers techniques from basic to advanced with practical examples across use cases.

3. **"Co-Intelligence" — Ethan Mollick (2024)**
   - *Why Read:* Wharton professor's guide to working with AI as a thinking partner. Practical and research-backed. Great for professionals applying AI to their work.

4. **"The AI Advantage" — Thomas H. Davenport (2018, updated)**
   - *Why Read:* How companies are using AI to gain competitive advantage. Business-focused, practical case studies.

5. **"Human Compatible" — Stuart Russell (2019)**
   - *Why Read:* Berkeley AI professor's vision for safe AI development. Essential reading for understanding AI alignment — increasingly relevant.

6. **"Prediction Machines" — Ajay Agrawal, Joshua Gans, Avi Goldfarb (2018)**
   - *Why Read:* Economics of AI explained clearly. Reframes AI as a prediction technology and explains business implications.

7. **"The Alignment Problem" — Brian Christian (2020)**
   - *Why Read:* Deep dive into the challenges of making AI do what we actually want. Accessible and important.

8. **"Deep Learning" — Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville (2016)**
   - *Why Read:* The foundational textbook for deep learning. Free online. Heavy but authoritative for those who want the technical foundation.

9. **"Atlas of AI" — Kate Crawford (2021)**
   - *Why Read:* Critical perspective on the social, political, and environmental costs of AI. Balances the techno-optimism with important grounding.

10. **"Superforecasting" — Philip Tetlock (2015)**
    - *Why Read:* Not AI-specific, but essential for thinking clearly about uncertain futures — a key skill for AI practitioners and strategists.
